---

## Committee Findings
The Committee reviewed the institution’s narrative and supporting documentation demonstrating where it publishes its accreditation status on its website, its catalog, and in multiple handbooks.   The institution reported that it has no branch campuses.  However, based on the supporting documentation, the Committee reviewed, the accreditation statement appears not to match the wording in SACSCOC’s Institutional Obligations for Public Disclosure policy.   For example, the accreditation statement in the catalog appears to be missing the second sentence, and the other documentation used a hyperlink to the SACSCOC website rather than publishing the SACSCOC website address.  Therefore, the Committee could not determine if the institution accurately represents its accreditation status and publishes the website address of SACSCOC.

## Response Narrative
The Committee requested additional evidence demonstrating the institution accurately represents its accreditation status and publishes the SACSCOC website address in accordance with the SACSCOC Institutional Obligations for Public Disclosure policy. The institution reviewed all published accreditation statements and made the necessary revisions to ensure each publication is consistent with the requirements of the SACSCOC Institutional Obligations for Public Disclosure policy. The following exhibits demonstrate the revised statements in the institution's official publications including the VSCC website,[cite](/documents/14-1/10_VSCC_Website.pdf "VSCC Website") the VSCC Catalog[cite](/documents/14-1/20_VSCC_Catalog.pdf "VSCC Catalog") in the “About VSCC”[cite](/documents/14-1/30_About_VSCC.pdf "About VSCC Section") section, the VSCC Employee Handbook,[cite](/documents/14-1/40_VSCC_Employee_Handbook.pdf "VSCC Employee Handbook") the VSCC Student Handbook,[cite](/documents/14-1/50_VSCC_Student_Handbook.pdf "VSCC Student Handbook") and both the Faculty[cite](/documents/14-1/60_VSCC_Faculty_Handbook.pdf "VSCC Faculty Handbook") and Adjunct Faculty[cite](/documents/14-1/70_VSCC_Adjunct_Handbook.pdf "VSCC Adjunct Faculty Handbook") Handbooks.
