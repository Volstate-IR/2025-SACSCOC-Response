## Committee Findings
The Committee reviewed the current Program Participation Agreement and the Eligibility and Certification Approval Report to determine compliance with the institution’s program responsibilities under Title IV of the most recent Higher Education Act and requirements for auditing financial aid programs.  The Program Participation Agreement is valid through 2025, and the Eligibility and Certification Approval Report was most recently approved in July 2014.  These materials demonstrate that the institution remains eligible to participate in Title IV programs.

The institute also provided documentation referencing prior federal and state financial aid auditing activity, including a systemwide federal financial aid review conducted in June 2022 and a state financial aid audit conducted by the Tennessee Student Assistance Corporation (TSAC) in July 2022.   However, the Committee could not locate documentation demonstrating more recent federal or state financial aid audits.   Therefore, the Committee could not determine whether the institution audits compliance with federal and state financial aid programs.

## Response Narrative
The Committee requested additional evidence demonstrating the institution’s audits of compliance with federal and state financial aid programs. The institution’s most recent federal financial aid review was the Tennessee Board of Regents systemwide federal financial aid review conducted in June 2022. The institution is now providing documentation of its most recent Tennessee Student Assistance Corporation (TSAC) program review of state financial aid programs for the 2024–2025 academic year,[cite](/documents/13-6/10_TSAC_Closure.pdf "TSAC Closure Letter") conducted October 20–23, 2025, and the final audit letter from the Tennessee Comptroller of the Treasury,[cite](/documents/13-6/20_Audit_Finding_Report.pdf "Audit Finding Report") which provides additional evidence of the institution’s compliance with applicable federal financial aid requirements. The TSAC review examined student eligibility, disbursements, satisfactory academic progress, program reconciliation, and institutional liability. The review identified no substantive discrepancies, and TSAC formally closed the review with no findings.
