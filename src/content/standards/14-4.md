## Committee Findings
The Committee found that, in addition to the institution’s accreditation by SACSCOC, the institution is accredited by four programmatic accrediting agencies recognized by the U.S. Department of Education: the Accreditation Commission for Education in Nursing (ACEN), the Commission on Accreditation in Physical Therapy Education (CAPTE), the Commission on Dental Accreditation (CODA), and the Joint Review Committee on Education in Radiologic Technology (JRCERT). The institution stated that it is in good standing with all four agencies and that no actions requiring notification of a change in accreditation status have occurred. The institution provided evidence of its standing with JRCERT in the form of the most recent action letter and self-study.

However, the Committee could not locate supporting evidence that it represents itself accurately to all U.S. Department of Education recognized accrediting agencies with which it holds accreditation or that it informs those agencies of any change of accreditation status, including the imposition of public sanctions. Furthermore, the institution did not provide copies of its most recent self-studies from these agencies or documentation confirming that no adverse actions were taken against its programs. Therefore, the Committee could not determine that the institution represents itself accurately to all recognized accrediting agencies and informs them of changes to its accreditation status.

## Response Narrative
The Committee requested additional evidence demonstrating the institution accurately represents its accreditation status to all U.S. Department of Education-recognized programmatic accrediting agencies and informs those agencies of any change in accreditation status, including the imposition of public sanctions. The institution provides the most recent accreditation action letters for all of its programmatically accredited programs. These letters demonstrate the institution remains in good standing with each accrediting agency. During the current accreditation cycle, the institution has not experienced a change in accreditation status or the imposition of a public sanction by SACSCOC or any programmatic accrediting agency. Accordingly, no notifications to other recognized accrediting agencies were required under this standard.
