## Committee Findings
The Committee reviewed the institution’s narrative and supporting documentation describing how catalogs are published on the institution’s website. The institution stated the current and prior versions of the catalog (beginning with 2007-2008) may be accessed on the institution’s website and that the print copies of catalogs from 1972 to 2012 are located in the registrar’s office and the Thigpen library.

However, the Committee could not locate the archived official catalogs from earlier academic years or evidence demonstrating how former and returning students access previous catalogs.  The supporting documentation provided included the (2025-2026) catalog information.  Therefore, the Committee could not determine that the institution ensures the availability of archived official catalogs sufficient to serve former and current students.

## Response Narrative
The Committee requested additional evidence demonstrating the availability of archived official catalogs sufficient to serve former and current students. The institution maintains archived official catalogs on its online catalog website, where current, former, and returning students may access previous catalog editions by selecting the desired academic year from the Catalog Year drop-down menu. An expanded view of the drop-down menu illustrates the archived catalog years available to current, former, and returning students, covering catalog years starting with 2007.[cite](/documents/10-3/10_Catalog_Dropdown.pdf "Catalog Year drop-down")  Official print catalogs published between 1972 and 2012 are maintained in the Office of Records and Registration and the Thigpen Library and are available upon request.  The front page of an archived catalog accessed via the drop-down menu is also shown.[cite](/documents/10-3/20_Catalog_Prior_Year.pdf "Archived catalog front page")
