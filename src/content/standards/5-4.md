## Committee Findings
The Committee reviewed the organizational chart identifying the institution’s administrative and academic officers, along with a table that included position descriptions, resumes or CVs, transcripts or Certifications, and relevant experience.  The Committee determined that the institution employes administrative and academic officers with credentials appropriate to the duties and responsibilities associated with their positions.

Additionally, the institution’s narrative described an annual evaluation process for administrative and academic officers as part of the Employee Performance Management System, including self-evaluations, supervisor evaluations, and the evaluation of professional competencies.  The institution also provided a sample of completed evaluations for two administrative and two academic officers for a single year.   Therefore the Committee could not determine that the institution regularly evaluates all administrative and academic officers.
